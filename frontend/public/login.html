About This System

What is LandVerify ZM?

LandVerify ZM is the Republic of Zambia's official digital land registry portal, established under the Ministry of Lands and Natural Resources to modernise and secure land administration across all ten provinces.

The system enables authorised land officers to register parcels, process ownership transfers, manage title deeds, and maintain an accurate national land record — all within a single, fully audited environment.

All records held in this system are legally authoritative and governed by the Lands Act, Chapter 184 of the Laws of Zambia. The system is powered by ARMS — the Automated Registry Management System — the Ministry's internal processing engine.
